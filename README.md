# Cinema Marketplace

Cinema Marketplace is a standalone commercial marketplace for stories, content partnerships, Digital Box Office opportunities, and brand collaborations.

## Scope and safety

- This repository is separate from the existing Cinema website repository.
- Prototype data is illustrative and must be labelled DEMO.
- No production Cinema database, authentication, secrets, or live integrations are connected.
- Google and other partner integrations remain disconnected until their owners authorize secure OAuth/API access.
- Commercial rights and availability must be verified before an opportunity is represented as available.

## Product direction

A Cinema-branded marketplace for brands, agencies, studios, publishers, rights holders, and creators to discover opportunities, submit briefs, and manage enquiries.
